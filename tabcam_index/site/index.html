For guitar and bass players

A camera for your guitar tabs

Scan the tabs you write, learn from and own. Record yourself playing them. Share them with the people you play with.

Open tabs.cam

Runs in your browser, on your phone or your computer.

01

Scan your tabs

Point your phone at a tab. tabs.cam finds the page, straightens it and reads it into text, so you find it and use it in seconds.

  • Reads the tab into plain text you can edit
  • Search by title, artist or the notes in the tab
  • On every device you sign in on

02

Record your practice

Cam shows the tab under the camera while you play, one phrase at a time, and keeps every take with its tab in the video.

  • Next phrase with a tap, a key or a pedal
  • Trim the take, mix in a song, add a title and fades
  • Download the video, ready to post

03

Share and play along

Send a tab's link to your students and friends. They get the tab with its recordings and videos, and play along with them.

  • One link, read only: your tab stays as you made it
  • Play a recording or a video above the tab as you play
  • What they record goes to their own copy

Start with the tab on your music stand

tabs.cam comes with no songs: everything in it is what you scan or add yourself, kept in your own account. No ads, no tracking.
