TabScan is a scanner app made for guitar and bass tablature. Point your phone at a tab and TabScan finds the page, straightens it and cleans it up. Then it reads the tab into plain-text tab you can edit, keep in sync and share.

SCAN LIKE A DOCUMENT SCANNER
• The system document scanner finds the page's edges and straightens it, several pages at a time.
• Add photos and videos from your library, files, or a link (a lesson video, a backing track).
• The Tab filter turns each page into crisp black ink on white; switch to the Original photo any time.
• Swipe and zoom through the pages, rotate them, add more or delete them.

READ TABS INTO TEXT
• Every capture is read by itself into plain-text tab, on your phone.
• Works with tabs printed from the web (e|--0--3--|) and with typeset tab staves from books and printouts, for six-string guitar and four-string bass.
• Fix what the reading got wrong right in the Text view, copy it, and mark it correct once you've checked it. Your edits are never overwritten.
• TabScan Premium subscribers also get their pages transcribed in the cloud as they sync.

ORGANIZE AND SYNC
• Your captures are events in a grid, newest first, with a title and artist. Rename, clone or delete them.
• Sign in with Google to sync your events across your phones and the web app, deletions included.
• Hide a capture's photos so only the text tab, videos and links show.

SHARE
• Share a capture as a link, a PDF, plain text, or a zip of all its files.
• Share a capture with people by their email address, to edit together or read only. They find it in "Shared with me" when they sign in. A link opens only for the people you shared with.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• You can scan without an account: signed out, everything stays on your device.
• Reading tabs, syncing and sharing need you signed in with Google.
• No ads, no tracking. You can delete your account and its cloud data from the Profile tab.

TabScan is for your own tabs: your notes, lesson sheets, books and printouts you own.
