TabCam scans guitar and bass tabs and reads them into editable plain-text tab.

Signing in: the app opens on a welcome screen with Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google; scanning and everything else need signing in. Sign in with Apple works with any Apple ID, and Google sign-in with any Google account; there's nothing else to set up.

What to try:
1. Capture tab > Scan: point the camera at any printed guitar tab. The system document scanner finds and straightens the page. The new capture is first on Home.
2. Open the capture: tap a page to see it, switch Original / Scan, Rotate. The Text tool shows the tab, read on the device in a few seconds: edit it (overtype, - and + on a fret), Copy it, and tap Verified.
3. Cam (the last tool): the camera with the tab under it, one line at a time; tap the line, or nod, to go to the next. Rec records a take, added to the capture as a video. The app asks for the camera and microphone here.
4. Share (the link icon) copies the capture's link. Opened anywhere, signed in or not, it shows the capture, read only.
5. Capture tab > Document, Photos & videos, Files or Link makes a capture from them.
6. Profile > Delete account deletes the account and its cloud data, and signs out.

The app has no in-app purchases and no links or buttons to buy anything. Some accounts have TabCam Premium (cloud transcription), a subscription not sold in the app; everything above works without it.
