TabCam scans guitar and bass tabs and reads them into editable plain-text tab.

About guideline 5.2.1 (third-party lyrics and sheet music): TabCam is a practice tool for musicians practicing with their own tabs, and it comes with no content of its own. It has no catalog, no songs, no tabs, no sheet music and no lyrics, and it doesn't download or fetch any. Everything in the app is user generated: what each user captures with the camera or adds from their own device (tabs they wrote, their teacher's lesson sheets, books and printouts they bought), kept in their own private account. The app says so where content is added: Capture shows "TabCam is for musicians practicing with their own tabs. Only add what you have the right to use: tabs you wrote, your teacher's, ones you bought, or ones free to share." A link a user adds is only a link, opened in the browser; this version no longer fetches pages from tab websites into the app. Every tab in the screenshots and in the steps below ("Amber Lane", "Blues Shuffle in A"…) is an exercise made up for them, not a published song.

Signing in: the app opens on a welcome screen with Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google; scanning and everything else need signing in. Sign in with Apple works with any Apple ID, and Google sign-in with any Google account; there's nothing else to set up.

What to try:
1. Capture tab > Scan: point the camera at any printed guitar tab. The system document camera finds and straightens the page. The new capture is first on Home.
2. Open the capture: tap a page to see it, switch Original / Scan, Rotate. The Text tool shows the tab, read on the device in a few seconds: edit it (overtype, - and + on a fret), Copy it, and tap Verified.
3. Cam (the last tool): the camera with the tab under it, one line at a time; tap the line, or nod, to go to the next. Rec records a take, added to the capture as a video. The app asks for the camera and microphone here.
4. Share (the link icon) copies the capture's link. Opened anywhere, signed in or not, it shows the capture, read only.
5. Capture tab > Document, Photos & videos, Files or Link makes a capture from them.
6. Profile > Delete account deletes the account and its cloud data, and signs out.

The app has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no links or buttons to buy anything: everything above is free.
