TabCam is a scanner app made for guitar and bass tablature. Point your phone at a tab and TabCam finds the page, straightens it and cleans it up. Then it reads the tab into plain-text tab you can edit, play along with and share.

SCAN LIKE A DOCUMENT SCANNER
• The system document scanner finds the page's edges and straightens it, several pages at a time.
• Add PDFs, photos and videos from your library, files, recordings, or a link (a lesson video, a backing track).
• The Tab filter turns each scanned page into crisp black ink on white; switch a page back to the Original photo any time.
• Zoom into pages, rotate them, reorder, rename or delete files, and add more.

READ TABS INTO TEXT
• Every capture is read by itself into plain-text tab, on your phone, with the page's chord names, lyrics and section names where they were.
• Works with tabs printed from the web (e|--0--3--|) and with typeset tab staves from books and printouts, for six-string guitar and four-string bass.
• Correct the reading in the Text view, with tools made for tab: overtype, raise or lower a fret, move a note to another string. Copy the tab, and tap Verified once you've checked it. Your edits and verified tabs are never overwritten.
• TabCam Premium subscribers also get their pages transcribed in the cloud as they sync.

PLAY ALONG WITH CAM
• Cam shows the camera with the tab under it, one line of the staff at a time. Go to the next line with a tap, any key, a Bluetooth page-turner pedal or a nod of your head.
• Record yourself playing along: each take is saved to the capture, with the tab in the video.
• Play one of the capture's recordings, videos or links above the camera while you play.

ORGANIZE AND SYNC
• Your captures show in a grid, newest first, with a title and artist. Search them by title, artist or the tab's text. Rename, clone or delete them.
• Your captures sync across your phones and the web app, deletions included.
• Download a whole capture as a zip, or any file on its own.

SHARE
• Share copies the capture's link, to send to anyone: whoever has it can see the capture, read only. Signed in, it stays with them, in "Shared with me".

YOUR ACCOUNT
• Sign in with Apple or with Google to start: your captures are kept on every device you sign in on.
• No ads, no tracking. Delete your account and its cloud data from the Profile tab at any time.
• In English, Spanish and Portuguese.

TabCam is for your own tabs: your notes, lesson sheets, books and printouts you own.
